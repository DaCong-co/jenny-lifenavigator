# Trademark Policy

The Jenny source code is free software, released under the AGPL-3.0 license
(see [LICENSE](LICENSE)). This policy covers what the license does not: the
project's name and visual identity.

## What is covered

- The name **"Jenny"** as it identifies this project
- The **Jenny logo and mascot artwork**
- The **flagDiZero** name and logo

These remain the property of the project owner and are **not** licensed under
the AGPL-3.0.

## What you can do without asking

- Use the code under the terms of the AGPL-3.0, including modifying and
  redistributing it.
- Refer to the project by name in a descriptive, truthful way: articles,
  reviews, tutorials, academic work ("built with Jenny", "a fork of Jenny",
  "compatible with Jenny").
- Keep the name in unmodified builds you compile yourself from this
  repository.

## What requires a different name

If you distribute a **modified** version of Jenny, you must:

- Ship it under a different name and logo, so users are not confused about
  what they are installing and who maintains it;
- Not use "Jenny", "flagDiZero", or the mascot artwork in your app name,
  package id, icon, store listing, or domain name;
- You may (and are encouraged to) state that your project is *based on*
  Jenny.

## What is never allowed

- Implying endorsement, affiliation, or official status without written
  permission;
- Using the name or logo in a way that suggests your build is the official
  one.

## Questions

Open an issue or contact the maintainer. Good-faith uses are almost always
fine — this policy exists to protect users from confusion, not to stop the
community from talking about the project.
